08 / Methodology
Census polygons are transformed from WGS 84 longitude/latitude into the selected raster CRS. Raster pixel centres are tested against the transformed AOI polygons.
Built-up surface is calculated by summing positive GHSL raster values. The conversion assumes values are square metres per cell. Verify the product documentation, scale factor and nodata value before official use.
GHSL 2025 and 2030 epochs must be labelled according to their product status. Do not present modelled or projected epochs as direct observations.
