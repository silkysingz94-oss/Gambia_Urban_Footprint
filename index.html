Methodology
The short-name GeoTIFFs are used directly and are expected to be in EPSG:4326. Census EA polygons are also in WGS 84. No EPSG:54009 reprojection is applied.

Raster pixel centres are tested against the selected Census geography. Positive built-up values are summed and converted from square metres to square kilometres.

The analysis uses the selected geography only. 2025 and 2030 results should be interpreted according to the source product's temporal definitions and must not automatically be treated as observed measurements.
