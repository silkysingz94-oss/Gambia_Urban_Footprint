DATA & METHODOLOGY
Dataset: Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL), GHS-BUILT-S R2023A.

Nominal resolution: 100 metres.

CRS: Automatically detected from GeoTIFF metadata.

Vector source: Census_EAs_Geo.json.

Analysis: Sum of positive built_surface pixel values within selected geography, converted from m² to km².

Epochs: 1975–2030. Later epochs must be interpreted according to the GHSL product metadata.
