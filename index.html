Methodology & interpretation
Census EA geometries are supplied in WGS 84. The raster coordinate system is detected from its extent. Where the raster uses EPSG:54009, AOI geometries are projected from EPSG:4326 to EPSG:54009 before pixel selection.

Positive pixel values are summed as built-up surface in square metres and converted to km². Pixel centres are tested against the selected AOI. The resulting area is an estimate based on included raster cells.

2025 and 2030 products should be interpreted according to the GHSL product documentation; do not automatically treat future/modelled epochs as observed measurements.
