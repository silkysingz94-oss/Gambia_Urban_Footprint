DATA & METHODOLOGY
Built-up dataset: Global Human Settlement Layer (GHSL), GHS-BUILT-S R2023A.

Spatial resolution: 100 metres.

Raster projection: World Mollweide, EPSG:54009.

Vector source: Census_EAs_Geo.json.

Analysis: Sum of positive built_surface pixel values within the selected administrative geography, converted from m² to km².

Historical periods: 1975–2020. 2025 and 2030 should be interpreted according to the GHSL product's epoch metadata and scenario status.

Data source: European Commission Joint Research Centre.
